Entry for the [make.fun bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/u-email-baru-make-the-judge-laugh-in-one-line-791ccb21): one funny line, 200 characters or fewer, judged head to head by a judge model.

## The line

> I asked my smart fridge for life advice and it said: stop standing here with the door open, staring into the cold empty dark — that is literally my job.

## How it works

`main.ts` (Node 22 standard library only) assembles the line from its named beats, validates the gate (1–200 characters, a single non-blank line), writes it to `/output/entry.txt`, and reads it back to verify the sha256.

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=/tmp/out node main.ts
```
